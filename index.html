GLITCH_CITY AESTHETIC

This generative algorithm processes procedural skylines according to elements observed in the reference schematics:

  • Monochrome Palette: Uses strict binary contrasts of black, white, and intermediate gray dither vectors.
  • Asymmetry Bias: Synthesizes massive black transmission blocks on the left half to offset the light-gray spires on the right.
  • Vertical Bleed: Extrudes a horizontal strip at the horizon vertically downwards, simulating water reflection or pixel sorting.
  • Slice Tearing & Blocks: Applies random horizontal slice displacements and rectangular noise blocks.
